# Prompt Log

## 1. My Prompt

I am a second-year CIS student at Africa University building a personal profile 
page for my Web Technologies assignment. Write me a short About Me paragraph of 
2-3 sentences that mentions my programme, my university, and why I am interested 
in technology. Keep it simple and not too formal.

## 2. AI Raw Output

Nothando Vimbai is a dedicated second-year Computer Information Systems student 
at Africa University, Zimbabwe, with a strong passion for leveraging technology 
to address community challenges. Committed to excellence in software engineering, 
Nothando continuously strives to develop innovative digital solutions that make 
a meaningful impact.

## 3. My Edited Version

My name is Nothando and I study Computer Information Systems at Africa University. 
I chose CIS because I like solving problems and I want to build apps one day.

## 4. Reflection

The AI wrote in third person which felt strange for a personal profile page so I 
changed it to first person. It also used phrases like "leveraging technology" and 
"meaningful impact" which sounded too formal and not like me at all. I kept the 
main idea but rewrote it in simpler words that actually sound like how I would 
describe myself.
