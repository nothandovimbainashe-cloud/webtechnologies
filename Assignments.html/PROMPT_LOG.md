# Prompt Log

## 1. My Prompt

I am a second-year CIS student at Africa University building a personal profile 
page for my Web Technologies assignment. Write me a short About Me paragraph of 
2-3 sentences that mentions my programme, my university, and why I am interested 
in technology. Keep it simple and not too formal.

## 2. AI Raw Output

Nothando Vimbai is a dedicated second-year Computer Information Systems student 
at Africa University, Zimbabwe, with a strong passion for leveraging technology 
to address community challenges. Committed to excellence in software engineering, 
Nothando continuously strives to develop innovative digital solutions that make 
a meaningful impact.

## 3. My Edited Version

My name is Nothando and I study Computer Information Systems at Africa University. 
I chose CIS because I like solving problems and I want to build apps one day.

## 4. Reflection

The AI wrote in third person which felt strange for a personal profile page so I 
changed it to first person. It also used phrases like "leveraging technology" and 
"meaningful impact" which sounded too formal and not like me at all. I kept the 
main idea but rewrote it in simpler words that actually sound like how I would 
describe myself.

# Prompt Log – Entry 2: Styling and Fixes

## 1. My Prompt

I asked Claude why my page showed plain text with no styling when I used
Go Live, and how to push my work to GitHub. Later I asked it to help me
write a new stylesheet in a maroon colour scheme, and then gave it my own
maroon CSS and asked it to "work with this styling and make necessary changes."
I also asked it to find the errors in my HTML.

## 2. AI Output (summary)

- It found that my stylesheet was missing from my project folder, which is
  why the page had no styling.
- It walked me through writing a new stylesheet part by part, explaining
  what each property does.
- It fixed problems in my maroon CSS: menu links that were nearly invisible
  on hover (red on maroon), a header that wasn't centred, sections touching
  the screen edges on phones, and the message box using a different font.
- It found errors in my HTML: a duplicate header tag, a missing closing
  header tag, an extra "<" before main, a list closed with <ul> instead
  of </ul>, and missing Projects and Contact sections.

## 3. My Edited Version

I kept my own maroon colours (#800000 and #cc0000) and layout. I chose the
colour scheme and added the university logo myself. I kept my original
About Me wording from entry 1 instead of the reworded version.
